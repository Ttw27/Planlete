## 21_after_week_four — B2C
```
Nobody talks about day 29.

Every plan sells you four or twelve weeks and then goes quiet about what happens next. You finish, and you're back to guessing.

## 22_the_questions — B2C
```
Three questions and a "personalised" label isn't personalisation.

Age, weight, goal — then the same template as everyone else with your name on top.

The questions that actually change a plan are the boring ones. Which days can you REALLY train. What's actually in your gym. What hurts. When do you play.

Use the answers or don't ask the questions.

#personalisedtraining #trainingplan #fitnessuk #gymuk #workoutplan

## 23_three_days — B2C
```
Three days a week is enough. The problem is what people do with them.

Taking a five-day split and doing three of the days isn't a three-day programme — it's a five-day programme with holes in it.

A proper three-day block is its own shape. Full body or upper/lower, compounds carrying the load, accessories chosen for what's actually missing.

Three well-built days beats five improvised ones every time.

#3daysplit #strengthtraining #gymuk #trainingplan #fitnessuk #lifting
```

## 24_missed_session — B2C
```
You missed Monday. The block is not ruined.

What ruins it is trying to cram two sessions into one, or bolting the missed volume onto Saturday, or writing off the week and "starting fresh Monday."

Shift the week forward, or skip it and carry on. Keep the hard sessions spaced. That's it.

Consistency over four weeks beats perfection over four days.

#gymmotivation #consistency #trainingtips #fitnessuk #strengthtraining
```

## 25_home_gym — B2C
```
A rack, a bar and a bench is more than enough. Genuinely.

Most people are limited by their programming, not their equipment. One kettlebell will get you a real four-week block if it's written properly.

The point is telling the plan what you actually have — so it's built around your garage, not a commercial gym plan with substitutions bolted on afterwards.

#homegym #garagegym #minimalequipment #strengthtraining #gymuk #kettlebell
```

## 26_deload — B2C
```
Week 4 going easier isn't you slacking. It's where the adaptation actually shows up.

Weeks 1–3 the load climbs — and so does fatigue, quietly. By week 3 you're strong and tired at the same time, and the fitness is hiding behind the tiredness.

Drop the volume, let the fatigue clear, and suddenly you can see what you've actually built. Then the next block starts from a real number.

#deload #periodisation #progressiveoverload #strengthtraining #fitnesseducation
```

## 27_logging — B2C
```
If you're not logging, you're guessing every single week.

You can't progressively overload a number you didn't write down. That's not a discipline thing, it's arithmetic.

Two minutes a session gets you: what to beat today, proof it's working when week three feels flat, and a real starting point for your next block.

Logging's built into the plan itself — not a second app you'll stop opening by Thursday 📝

#workoutlog #traininglog #progressiveoverload #gymtips #strengthtraining
```

## 28_long_layoff — B2C

Start deliberately low, let the load climb, build the habit before the numbers. You'll be back where you were faster by starting slower.
