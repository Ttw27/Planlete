## 1. Positioning

**One-liner (use everywhere):**
> A proper 4-week training block, built around you, for one payment. No subscription.

**The three things every post should ladder back to:**
1. It's personalised — actually, not "we asked your age and picked a template"
2. It explains itself — every exercise tells you why it's there
3. You pay once — you own it

**What we do NOT claim:** specific weight loss, specific timelines, medical outcomes, or injury cure. See §8.

## 8. Honest gaps — read this bit

**1. You have no social proof.** Everything above works around that, but it's a real ceiling. Your fastest fix: give the product free to 5–10 coaches or physios in exchange for a filmed 30-second reaction. Do it in week one. Real faces beat everything in this list.

**2. Health claims and the ASA.** UK advertising rules apply to organic social, not just paid ads. I've deliberately kept every script clear of outcome claims — no weight-loss numbers, no timelines, no "fix your injury." Keep it that way, and be especially careful with the rehab and physio content. If you improvise on camera, that's where you'll slip.

**3. Your legal suite hasn't had solicitor review yet.** You flagged that as outstanding. Driving traffic before that review is the risk you're actually taking here — not the content. Worth deciding consciously rather than by default.

**4. Branding is unmatched.** None of this reflects your actual colours, type or site voice, because I couldn't see the site. Send me the homepage copy and hex codes and I'll rewrite the captions to match and spec the carousel templates properly.

**5. Volume is the real job.** 22 posts is roughly a month. If it works you'll need this again every month, and the second month has to be built from what actually performed. Check your analytics after week two — you've already got page-view and sample-plan-view tracking in place, so tag the bio link per platform and you'll know which of the two audiences is actually biting.
